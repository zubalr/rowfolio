# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants in this project — issues, discussions, code review, and any
other project space — are expected to behave professionally and treat others
with respect. We are committed to a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, ability, language, or level of
experience.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and assume good faith.
- Critique code and decisions, not people.
- Accept corrective feedback gracefully and offer it kindly.
- Keep discussions on topic and productive.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or demeaning language of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Any other conduct a reasonable participant would consider inappropriate in
  a professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within all project spaces (repository, issue
tracker, discussions, chat) and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers, preferably via a private
GitHub contact or the security-advisory channel if confidentiality matters.
Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently exclude participants who violate this policy. Repeated or severe
violations lead to permanent exclusion. Enforcement decisions and their
reasons are documented privately by the maintainers; reports are handled
confidentially.

## Attribution

This policy is an original, concise code of conduct maintained by this
project's maintainers. Communities with different needs are encouraged to
adopt the policy that fits them.
